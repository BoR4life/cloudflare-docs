The mark is licensed for a different project, not for company identity

The artwork is Jullien’s existing piece Us. It was not commissioned for Bundle of Rays. The permission on record, from Studio Jean Jullien on 02 Jun 2025, reads: “Jean is happy to let you use US, It would have to be limited to this project only, ie not used again over consecutive conferences etc. If you’re ok with that, no fee for you.” The project was The Just Us Show — a monthly VR gathering for nurses and teachers.

The studio then asked how long the show would run. The reply proposed a year, or an initial six months, and raised using it “potentially as an implied logo for the show”. There is no further reply on record — neither the term nor the logo use was ever agreed.

What this kit assumes differs on every axis: a different entity (Bundle of Rays Academy Pty Ltd, not a community show), indefinite use, trademark use as company identity, and a derivative work, since a “BUNDLE OF RAYS” wordmark has been added beneath the artwork. None of that was granted.

Next step: ask Studio Jean Jullien for a written licence covering company identity — scope, territory, term, permitted media (print, digital, garments, XR textures), and whether the wordmark lockup is allowed as a derivative. Expect a fee; the free grant was tied to one community project. If they decline, commission a new mark. Nothing here should be printed, registered or shipped until that is settled.

Everything below is a sound system for this artwork and worth keeping — but it is contingent on that licence.

The premise

The mark is the constraint, not a decoration on top of one

Jullien draws with one black ink brush pen and fills flat block colour in digitally. That method is the whole system: ink and paper do the work, colour arrives flat and unmixed, nothing blends. A brush-ink logo cannot sit on a gradient, inside a glass card, or beside a glowing purple “AI” flourish without one of the two looking like a mistake — and it will not be the logo that looks wrong.

The strategic value: healthcare and university procurement decks are saturated with clinical blue, stock headset photography and circuit‑board overlays. A hand‑drawn ink mark on uncoated paper is instantly distinguishable in that room. Protect that.

02 · Typography

Don’t chase the hand‑lettering. Contrast with it.

The wordmark is drawn by hand. Nothing in a type library will match it, and the near‑miss looks worse than an honest contrast.

XR

All three faces are SIL OFL 1.1, and that is deliberate: you can embed them in Unity and Unreal builds without a licence problem. Commercial foundry licences routinely exclude embedding in an application binary — which is exactly what an XR module is. Don’t swap these for a paid face without reading the EULA’s embedding clause.

03 · Texture

Three textures, all of them from print

Because the mark comes from ink. Each panel below is the real effect, not a description of it.

Paper grain Full‑bleed grounds only, 0.045 opacity. Higher than that and it eats small type. Drop it entirely in‑headset — at foveated resolutions it turns to shimmer.

Drawn edge

Brush edge Rough edges on containers and dividers, derived from the mark’s own stroke. Rules are 2.5px, not hairlines. Corner radius 2px — the mark has no soft corners.

Misregister

Risograph offset A 2px offset of a flat behind black type. Covers and titles, sparingly. Reads as printed and human — it is the treatment on this page’s own title.

Prohibited without exception

  • gradients
  • drop shadows
  • glassmorphism
  • glow
  • bevel
  • circuit‑board overlays
  • stock headset photography

Every one of them fights the mark and dates the brand to the vendor category you are trying to leave.

Never recolour the mark, never outline it, never add a strapline inside the lockup. The reversed panel above is simulated by inversion — a true reversed asset still needs to come from the studio.

Clear space and minimum size

Clear space on all four sides equals the cap height of the wordmark. Minimum reproduction is 24 mm wide in print, 96 px on screen — below that the eyes fill in and the forms go muddy.

05 · Open

Three things block sign‑off

Licence

The mark is not licensed for company use. The permission on record covers The Just Us Show only, with no fee and no ongoing use, and the wordmark lockup is an ungranted derivative. This is the blocker that outranks the other two — see the top of this page.

Blocker

The logo file is a 225 × 224 px raster. That is a web thumbnail, not a master. Any vector artwork has to come from Studio Jean Jullien — the same conversation as the licence, so do both at once. Before any print, signage, garment or XR‑texture use — anything above 96 px falls apart, and every application in this kit assumes artwork that does not exist yet.

Decision

The palette is proposed, not approved. Ray, Pulse, Breath and Deep are derived from Jullien’s flat‑colour method and pass contrast, but they are one reading of the mark. Sign them off or push back before anything is printed.
