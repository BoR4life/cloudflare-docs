# ACU joint venture governance and the PhD firewall

## The structural problem, stated plainly

ACU occupies **four** roles at once in Brad's world:

1. **30% owner** of Kindred Care — a commercial counterparty
2. **Ethics approver** — the HREC that governs the PhD, including Paper 5, which uses
   Kindred Care transcripts
3. **Academic supervisor's employer** — Prof Robert Tai (CI) and the co-supervisors
4. **Potential go-to-market channel** — nursing schools, clinical networks, credibility

Brad occupies three: CEO of the venture, Student Investigator on the ethics
application, and PhD candidate. He also holds an Innovation Fellowship at The Ohio
State University and runs Bundle of Rays.

None of this is improper. All of it is **declarable**, and the failure mode is not
misconduct — it is a decision made in the commercial track that turns out to have
consequences in the research track that nobody checked for. That check is this file.

## The firewall check

Run this **before** any commercial decision that touches data, participants,
publication, or ACU. Five questions. Any "yes" means the decision needs a
conversation before it is made, not after.

1. **Does it change what data is collected, from whom, or how?**
   → Consent and HREC coverage may no longer match reality. Research use of
   transcripts must be covered by the consent given *at the point of collection*.
   Retrofitted consent is a finding, and a serious one.

2. **Does it change who the users are?**
   → New cohorts, new sites, or a new jurisdiction can put participants outside the
   approved protocol. Overseas recruitment channels — including anything touching the
   Jaypee relationship — carry a standing COI mitigation requirement.

3. **Does it involve recruiting, incentivising, or paying anyone who is or could be a
   research participant?**
   → Commercial inducement to research participants is a direct conflict. Route it
   through the declared mitigation, or don't do it.

4. **Does it create a claim that a published paper would contradict, or vice versa?**
   → The deck, the grant application, the website, and P1–P7 are one story. A
   marketing claim the research cannot support damages both, and the research is the
   harder of the two to repair.

5. **Does it change ACU's commercial position, contribution, or exposure?**
   → That is a JV governance matter and goes to the ACU counterpart in writing, not
   through the supervisor. Keep the commercial conversation and the academic
   conversation in separate threads with separate people.

Record the outcome — the questions triggered, who was told, what was decided — in
`kindred-care/decisions.md`. The value of a firewall is entirely in the paper trail.

## Route it to the right office

ACU is not one counterparty. Sending a commercial question to the research office, or
an ethics question to the commercialisation office, produces delay and confusion, and
occasionally an answer from someone without authority to give it.

| Matter | Goes to |
|---|---|
| Protocol change, consent, participant safety | HREC, via the CI (Prof Robert Tai) |
| Supervision, publication sequence, thesis | Supervisory panel |
| JV terms, IP, equity, revenue | ACU commercialisation / the JV counterpart, in writing |
| Channel introductions, pilots in ACU networks | The commercial counterpart, logged in the board |

Where a matter is genuinely both — a pilot inside an ACU network that also produces
research data is the standard example — it goes to **both**, in writing, and the
board records both approvals before the pilot starts.

## Publication versus commercial confidentiality

These two will collide, and it is better to settle the rule before the first collision:

- The PhD requires publication. Investors and partners often prefer confidentiality.
- Agree in advance what Kindred Care will never ask the research to withhold — negative
  findings, in particular. A venture that suppresses its founder's negative findings is
  finished the day it is discovered, and in a small market it is always discovered.
- Where a paper will name Kindred Care, the COI declaration must be explicit and
  visible. Understating it is worse than the conflict itself.
- Where marketing will cite a paper, cite it accurately, link the source, and state the
  founder's role in the research on the same page as the claim.

## Standing rules

- **"Confirmed" means written.** Applies identically to panel members, co-authors,
  pilot sites, ACU commitments, and investors. Before written acceptance: *invited*,
  *approached*, *proposed*, *in discussion*.
- **One consistent title per person** across every document in a set.
- **Australian English, APA 7th** in anything academic-facing.
- **Any change to scope, roles, sequence, thresholds, or entity structure propagates.**
  List every affected document in the same session and tell Brad. Documents disagreeing
  with each other was the single most damaging critique finding on the PhD side; the
  same failure in a data room is more expensive.
- When Kindred Care work touches PhD documents directly, load the
  `phd-define-writing` skill as well — its conventions govern that material.
