# The ACU pathway — UCAC, the register, and the locks

This is the actual investment route. Everything on the investment track runs through it.

## The route

`Scope → Propose → Plan` (the frame-out stages), delivering into ACU's internal gates:

**UCAC assessment sitting → Venture Committee endorsement → Investment Committee submission**

UCAC is the ACU University Commercialisation Advisory Committee. It recommends a
Commercialisation Venture to the Vice-Chancellor for approval to the Investment
Committee. Nothing reaches Breakthrough Victoria without clearing that chain.

Funding cascade as documented: **~$300k POC co-investment converting to shares**, then
**BVUIP $1.0M matched 1:1** (phased $550k FY26 / $250k FY27 / $200k FY28), with CRC-P
Round 19 as the non-dilutive track and a conditional Series A of $1.5M–$2.5M at FY28.
No single document states the cascade end to end — see the reconciliation flags in
`canonical-facts.md`.

## The two structural blockers — both ACU-side, both on Brad's critical path

**1. The 1:1 cash match.** BVUIP requires matched cash. ACU has flagged that no cash
pool exists for the $250k–$500k match. Resolution paths on the register: routed joint
non-dilutive grants, or an HNWI syndicator through ACU Enterprise Office. Tasks P2-13,
P5-04, P5-08, MTG-04. **This is the deal.** Nothing else matters if it does not resolve.

**2. Heads of Agreement and the IP audit.** Required for both UCAC and BVUIP. Licence-
first with an assignment trigger; BoR platform licensed, not assigned, into NewCo;
foreground IP into Kindred Care Pty Ltd. Tasks P2-04, P2-05, P2-10, P4-09, MTG-03.

Neither is Brad's to solve and both gate him. That is the classic programme failure
mode — the gating items sit with people whose day jobs are elsewhere. Every checkpoint
must state the age of these two in days, by name, whether or not they moved.

## The locks — enforce these in every document

### Probity (locked in the register)

- **The institutional investor is never named** — internally or externally.
- **ACU is described as "active, multi-pathway partnership exploration"** until a formal
  agreement is executed. Never as a settled partner.
- **Every external ACU-naming document is sighted by Russell before release.** No
  exceptions, and log it.
- **No escalation to senior ACU leadership without coordination.** DVCs are approached
  by role and brokered through Russell.
- **VIC sites = Trial Partners** (pilot sites). **QLD / SCHHS = Evaluation Partners**
  (national signal). Do not blur them.
- COIs on the register — including Mel Barlow's supervisory role, which touches the PhD.

### Product language (locked in the register)

- Always **"the Companion"**. Never tool, platform, or app.
- Always **"midwifery- and nurse-led"**.
- **Education-only.** Stage 5d — symptom interpretation, triage, diagnosis — is never
  reintroduced. This is architecturally enforced (P4-03) and evidenced (P4-06, P3-09),
  and it is what protects the SaMD position. Any copy that drifts toward clinical
  language attacks the regulatory moat directly.
- "Patient education" has no budget line in a health service — **frame on workforce and
  equity**, never on patient education.

**GTM v2.1 predates the language lock and calls Kindred Care "an AI-enabled maternal
education platform" throughout.** Sweep it before it enters the pack.
