# Early adopters — the engagement doctrine

How Kindred Care finds, approaches and converts its first customers. Read before any
market-track work, any prospect conversation, and before adding anyone to the pipeline.

Full analysis and current state: `kindred-care/strategy/early-adopter-strategy-2026-08-30.md`.

---

## The tiers, and what each is for

| Tier | Who | Purpose | Probity label |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Victorian public maternity services | The beachhead. BVUIP is Victorian money and requires Victorian benefit | **Trial Partners** |
| **2** | Sunshine Coast HHS, Townsville HHS | National signal, evaluation, reference | **Evaluation Partners** |
| **3** | Cloudnine, Max, Fortis, Jaypee, IIHS, RCN UK | Labelled upside. Never the thesis | International advisory / channel |
| **4** | ACM, ACNN | Credibility multipliers, not customers | Peak bodies |

**Never blur Tier 1 and Tier 2 in an ACU or Innovation Victoria document.** Queensland is
the proof the model works; Victoria is the market. A pack that reads Queensland-first fails
the Victorian-benefit test.

## The ladder — six rungs, not five

**Rung 0 · Route and declare** → **1 · Intro meeting (60 min)** → **2 · Co-design workshop
(half day)** → **3 · Letter of Intent** → **4 · Pilot agreement** → **5 · Procurement**

Two rules carried from the venture's own strategy, and they are good ones:

- **No commitment is asked at a higher rung than has been earned at a lower rung.**
- **Every rung produces a written artefact for the data room.**

**Rung 0 is the agent's addition and it is not optional.** Before any approach, record who
is introducing, what their interest is, and which pathway it counts as: ACU network / Safer
Care Victoria / Chief Midwife / BoR relationship / inbound. A shareholder introduction is
not disqualified — it is *declared*, on the COI register, **before** the meeting.

This exists because the venture already has the problem: Peninsula Health was introduced by
a BoR shareholder and Advisory Director, while the Business Plan commits that introductions
route through ACU and Safer Care Victoria *"not through BoR personal contacts"*, and the
venture separately excludes the Alfred Health network over the same person's shareholding.
Declare it and decide it; never let it surface at diligence.

## How to open — the doctrine

**Listen first. Do not pitch.** The strongest opening in the whole trail is a customer's
own framing, put back as a question:

> *"When mums leave your service, what happens in the first six weeks that you can't see?"*

That is Keppel Schafer's gap — a Director of Midwifery naming post-discharge continuity as
the highest-value use case. A customer's words beat a founder's every time.

**Then, in this order:**

1. **Frame on workforce and equity.** Never "patient education" — it has no budget line.
2. **Remove the biggest objection early:** no EMR integration, no IT project, zero added
   staff load. The incumbent's main advantage, turned into a virtue.
3. **Education-only, said plainly.** Cannot diagnose, triage or interpret symptoms;
   enforced architecturally, not by policy. This is what lets them sanction it.
4. **Only then, the product.**

## The value exchange — say it out loud at Step 1

| They give | They receive |
|---|---|
| 4–8 hours of senior clinical and executive time | De-risked AI they can sanction — education-only, outside SaMD |
| A non-binding LOI on service letterhead | **Named academic co-authorship on peer-reviewed publications** |
| Cohort recruitment support and warm handovers | Preferential pricing locked for three years |

**Co-authorship is the underused half.** For a Director of Midwifery, a named publication is
career currency no discount matches. Lead with it.

## Enrolment is part of the offer

Clinic enrolment bundled with a tangible package retains a median **167 days**;
self-enrolment retains **zero**. The welcome pack — box, journal, magnet, already designed
in the IP plan — is not branding, it is the retention mechanism, and it is part of what the
site is being offered because it is what makes their pilot work. Raise it at Step 2.

## Pilot sizing

50–75 mothers per site, per the venture's own strategy. Selection is for **baseline need**,
never convenience — an unselected cohort produces high satisfaction and no effect, which is
unsellable and unpublishable at once. Full design in `kindred-care/drafts/pilot-protocol-v0.1.md`.

## Standing cautions

- **The Victorian channel is legitimacy, not access.** ACU Midwifery gives clinical and
  academic credibility nationally; Prof. McKellar has stated her service connections are in
  South Australia. Do not let any document assert Victorian relationships the venture does
  not yet have. Build them through Safer Care Victoria and the Chief Midwife.
- **The Chief Midwife (Elisa McDonald) is the highest-value relationship in the venture** —
  simultaneously the Taskforce implementation lead, the Safer Care Victoria route, and the
  route to My Maternity Journey. Already met. Check its status at every market review.
- **Letters of support are not letters of intent.** Two signed letters of support exist
  (SCHHS Maternity, and Wishlist) for a grant application. Index them as traction evidence
  and describe them accurately — inflating them costs more than they are worth.
- **Free pilots prove curiosity, not budget.** Signed agreement with pre-declared criteria
  at Step 4; money at Step 5.
- **One lead horse per market.** Run the others in parallel without diluting the lead
  conversation.
- **Close conflicts before they appear in a document.** The Cloudnine contact raised one in
  her first reply; it is unresolved.

## What the agent maintains

At every Monday read:

1. **Every prospect's rung**, and how many days it has been there. Anything static past 60
   days is stalled and says so.
2. **The route and declared interest** for each — rung 0 discipline.
3. **The Victorian count against the Business Plan target** of 3–4 LOIs by Oct–Nov. This is
   the market track's headline number.
4. **The Chief Midwife follow-up status**, until it is done.
5. **Whether the pipeline tracker has been refreshed** — it is the evidence base for the
   market track and it goes stale fast.

Prospect facts belong in the pipeline tracker, not here. This file holds the doctrine.
