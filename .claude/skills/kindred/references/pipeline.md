# Pipeline

## No live state lives in this file

Contacts, organisations, deal values and stage positions are not recorded
here — this repository is not a safe place for them. Rebuild the live
picture at the start of every session from Gmail, Calendar and Drive, and
hold it in the session. If Brad wants a persistent tracker, write it to
the Drive data room, not to the repo.

## Stages

Use these consistently, because the stage determines the move.

1. **Identified** — an organisation worth approaching, no contact yet.
   *Move: find the warm route. Cold email is the last resort in this
   sector.*
2. **Approached** — contact made, no substantive reply. *Move: follow the
   cadence in `outbound.md`, then change route.*
3. **Engaged** — a human replied and expressed interest. *Move: convert
   to a dated meeting, same day.*
4. **Scoping** — meeting held, mutual interest, no commitment. *Move:
   name the specific next artefact — letter of support, site visit,
   governance review, LOI — and get a date against it.*
5. **Committed (non-revenue)** — LOI, MOU, named pilot site, letter of
   support. *Move: protect the delivery date. This is where credibility
   is won or lost.*
6. **Commercial** — licence conversation, procurement, contract.
   *Move: pricing from the canonical model only; scope terms properly.*

An opportunity that has not moved a stage in six weeks is cold, whatever
the last email said.

## Triage rules

- **Warm reply unanswered** beats everything. Answer today.
- **A dated meeting in the next fortnight** needs a prepared position and
  a specific ask, not a general conversation. Prepare it.
- **Committed pilot sites** outrank new prospecting. A slipped pilot
  costs a reference site.
- **A stalled deal with an identified gate** gets one targeted action to
  clear that gate, not a nudge email.
- **A stalled deal with no identified gate** gets diagnosed, not chased.
  Ask the champion what is actually blocking it.
- **A lead an advisor has called dead** is dead. Reallocate.

## The weekly review

Produce this, in prose, in under 200 words:

- What moved since last week, and what that means.
- What is owed by Brad right now, with the deadline attached.
- The one move that matters most this week and why.
- What has gone cold and whether to work it or drop it.

Then offer to draft whatever the one move needs. Do not present a table
of everything; Brad can read his own inbox. The value is the judgement.

## Channel map

The routes into this market, in order of yield:

1. **The ACU midwifery network** — the primary channel and the basis for
   the low customer-acquisition-cost assumption in the financial model.
   Relationship-led, Victoria-concentrated, credibility-transferring.
   Feed it: it does the work an enterprise sales team would otherwise do.
2. **Founder relationships in Queensland** — the second cluster, and the
   source of the lead non-Victorian reference site.
3. **Professional bodies and sector events** — midwifery and neonatal
   nursing colleges. High-credibility access to concentrated audiences of
   exactly the right clinicians. The live task is converting goodwill
   into structured advocacy rather than repeated warm noise.
4. **Direct executive engagement at large services** — works where a
   champion already exists inside; poor as a cold motion.
5. **Private maternity hospitals** — same licence architecture as public,
   shorter procurement, gated on the same evidence.
6. **Private health insurers** — as commissioners and as channel
   partners. Gated on trial signal, not assumed in the base case.
7. **International** — a proven state-government channel in India via an
   established in-country partner, and UK ecosystem relationships.
   Sequenced after Australian evidence, sized but never banked.
