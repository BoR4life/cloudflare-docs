# Funding and grants

Non-dilutive funding is a sales motion with a different buyer. Same
discipline applies: know the gate, name the champion, never over-claim
maturity.

## The tracks

**State co-funding and industry programs (e.g. Advance Queensland PSP).**
Typically gated on a letter of support from a health service on its own
letterhead. That letter is the forcing function — verbal support is not
support. If the letter is not secured before the close date, defer rather
than submit thin: a weak submission burns the round and the relationship.

**University-linked investment programs (e.g. Breakthrough Victoria
BVUIP).** Requires matched cash, and the matching capacity is the live
risk — the university has flagged no cash pool for the match. Any work on
this track is contingent on a credible match pathway existing. Do not let
a submission timeline drive past that question; surface it early and
loudly.

**Federal research consortium tracks (e.g. CRC-P).** Runs in parallel as
a non-dilutive complement. Scored on consortium quality and IP retention,
so international advisory relationships must be positioned strictly as
non-partner advisors and the IP architecture must read as Australian-
retained. Letters of support from ecosystem partners are structured
deliberately, not collected ad hoc.

**Research and translation funders (e.g. CSIRO programs, MRFF).** Best
approached through the university's enterprise office rather than
directly — they know the round calendars and the assessor expectations.
Book the standing meeting; the value is in the calendar, not the pitch.

**R&D Tax Incentive.** A standing mechanism with no IP gate and no
competitive process. Register early so proof-of-concept spend is
captured. This is the least glamorous item on the list and frequently the
largest cash return — if registration has not happened, it is usually the
highest-value action available.

## How to frame Kindred Care for a funder

Funders buy the problem and the evidence gap, not the product. The frame:

- **The problem is measurable and unowned.** The postpartum cliff, the
  avoidable presentations, the equity gap for CALD, First Nations, rural
  and non-default journeys.
- **The intervention is governed and specific.** Academically validated
  content, retrieval-grounded rather than free-form, non-diagnostic,
  Australian-resident data.
- **The evidence is the deliverable.** Maternal confidence, wellbeing and
  avoidable presentations measured from day one. Grant money buys the
  evaluation, which is exactly what a funder wants to have funded.
- **The AI risk is named, not hidden.** Generative AI in education can
  worsen learning when it is designed for fluency over thinking. Kindred
  Care is scaffolded by design and measures metacognitive dependency in
  the evaluation. Naming this scores better than avoiding it — assessors
  are actively looking for teams that have thought about it.
- **Capital efficiency.** The channel is a relationship network rather
  than an enterprise sales force, which is why the acquisition-cost
  assumption is structurally low. State this qualitatively in public
  material; the modelled figures are confidential.

## Discipline

- Match the framing to the scheme's own criteria and language. Read the
  guidelines before drafting, every time.
- Include or exclude First Nations framing depending on the track — this
  is set per-funder on the university's instruction, not by preference.
- Never claim deployed, proven or in-service. Proof of concept climbing
  to validated prototype, with the validation as the funded work.
- Every submission naming the university is sighted by the
  commercialisation manager before it goes.
