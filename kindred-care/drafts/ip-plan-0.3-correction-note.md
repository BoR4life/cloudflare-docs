## Correction 1 — MATERIAL. The platform is BoR background IP, not ACU's

**Draft 0.2 lists under ACU background IP:**

> *"Multi-tenant AI Agent framework — Operational on GCP — ADK, Gemini, Vertex Search,
> Cloud Run; tenant isolation; admin UI; Looker analytics"*

**This is Bundle of Rays background IP.** It is the `bor-iridia` platform: four services
built by Hector Gonzalez, multi-tenant isolation with row-level security, admin console,
Cloud Run deployment, running in dev since before the ACU engagement began. Two later
documents have it correctly:

- **Business Plan v2 (Jul 2026), §5:** *"BoR contributes: AI platform architecture (GCP),
  CLARA AI engine, AIDA agent framework, persona engine, RAG integration, avatar
  orchestration, analytics dashboards, **multi-tenant infrastructure**, SSO, billing, all
  codebase and documentation. Remains BoR property."*
- **Product Overview v1.0 (Jun 2026):** *"**Bundle of Rays owns the underlying platform**;
  ACU's contribution rests on maternal content, ontology, governance and academic
  validation."*

**Corrected allocation for 0.3:**

| Asset | 0.2 says | 0.3 should say |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-tenant AI Agent framework (GCP, ADK, Gemini, Vertex Search, Cloud Run, tenant isolation, admin UI, analytics) | ACU background IP | **BoR background IP — licensed into the JV** |
| Top Ten Maternal Issues ontology | ACU background IP | **ACU** — unchanged, subject to ACU verification |
| ACU-approved maternal content (40-doc foundation) | ACU background IP | **ACU** — unchanged, subject to ACU verification |
| Academic governance processes | ACU background IP | **ACU** — unchanged, subject to ACU verification |
| Midwifery curriculum IP (TBC) | ACU background IP | **ACU** — unchanged, still TBC |

**Why this matters beyond accuracy.** The register's own rule is *"do not assert unverified
ACU IP — diligence liability."* Asserting BoR's platform **as** ACU IP is the more damaging
form of the same error: it misstates the contribution stack that the contribution-based cap
table is calculated from. If it reached the IP register (P2-05, due 18 Sep) or the Heads of
Agreement, it would understate BoR's contribution in the equity split and be expensive to
unwind. Correct it before the workshop, not after.

**Note for the workshop:** the ACU-side entries remain marked *"to be verified by ACU"* in
0.3. That discipline is right and should stay — it is the other half of the same rule.

## Correction 2 — The content-governance moat is rated for a future state

Draft 0.2 rates the content governance moat **"STRONG — TODAY"** on the basis of
*"four years of continuous ACU clinical governance over a maternal corpus."*

The corpus is the 40-document foundation the POC exists to build and validate. There is no
four-year governance trail today; there is a governance *design* and the first documents.

**Corrected rating:** *"BUILDING — STRONG BY FY29."* The argument is actually more
persuasive stated honestly, because it is a compounding asset: *"every month of signed-off
curation widens a gap a competitor cannot close by spending money — they would need the
same academic partnership and the same elapsed time."* A reviewer who catches an
overstatement discounts everything around it; a reviewer who sees a moat honestly dated
believes the date.

## Correction 4 — Two assets in 0.2 belong in the commercial documents

These are in the IP Plan and nowhere else. They are being under-used.

**The ten-avatar multicultural library** — First Nations, grandmother, peer-aged and CALD
figures, with a documented warmth and psychological-safety methodology. This is a direct,
demonstrable differentiator against the incumbent, and it is absent from the GTM's
competitive section and from the Steering Committee deck. It should appear in both.

**The physical welcome pack** — box, journal and magnet, listed under registered designs.
The international retention evidence is unambiguous that a tangible artefact at enrolment
is among the strongest predictors of sustained engagement: in the SMART start study,
clinic enrolment bundled with a physical package produced a median retention of 167 days
against zero for self-enrolment. **The welcome pack is not a branding nicety — it is a
retention mechanism**, and it should move into the pilot protocol and the cost model.

**Also worth surfacing:** the nine patentable concepts identified in the 2022 ipCapital
Group inventory. 0.3 should note which, if any, are Kindred Care-relevant — that is a
question for the workshop and it feeds the IP register.

## One term the investor will ask about

> *"Improvements to licensed IP flow back to the licensor and re-license to the JV."*

This means improvements to the BoR platform made with BVUIP money accrue to BoR, not to
the company Innovation Victoria has invested in. It is a common and defensible JV term —
the licensor maintains one codebase, the JV gets the benefit — but it will be asked, and
the answer should be prepared rather than improvised. Flag it for the workshop so the
position is agreed between the parties before it is put to an investor.
