**The context.** I'm CEO of Bundle of Rays. We are working with Australian Catholic

**The attached draft.** *The Ten Complications of Postnatal Care* — the complications that

ordered by consequence rather than frequency, with risk windows and sourced figures. I
wrote it as a nurse before I wrote it as anything else.

Its argument is one I suspect the College will recognise: most postnatal education is
organised around what is common, which produces staff confident with engorgement and
uncertain about a rising shock index. And when you read the risk windows in sequence
against a 24-to-48-hour discharge, almost the entire peak-risk period sits outside direct
clinical observation.

It is offered to the College to use, adapt or discard. There is no condition attached.

## ATTACHMENT — module outline to accompany the clinical reference

**Working title:** Recognising deterioration after discharge — a postnatal module for
midwives and nurses

**Rationale.** The risk windows for the complications that kill and seriously harm women
sit almost entirely after discharge. This module is organised by consequence and by risk
window rather than by frequency.

| Session | Content | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why frequency and consequence are different axes; reading a risk window; the discharge gap | 45 min |
| 2 | The time-critical four — postpartum haemorrhage, sepsis, VTE, hypertensive disorders. Compensated physiology, the shock index, the early signals | 90 min |
| 3 | The under-recognised four — perinatal mental illness, lactation failure and mastitis, iron deficiency and anaemia, wound morbidity | 90 min |
| 4 | The unasked two — bladder and bowel dysfunction, cardiac complications. Covert retention; why a woman who cannot lie flat needs an echo | 60 min |
| 5 | The neonatal counterpart, and the dyad as one follow-up problem | 45 min |
| 6 | *Optional, separable* — where supervised AI does and does not belong in postnatal follow-up. Written to be dropped without weakening the module | 45 min |

**Note on session 6.** It is deliberately last and deliberately optional. If the College
would rather the module carried no AI content at all, that is a fine outcome and the
module is complete without it.
