# Kindred Care — Victorian pilot protocol

**Draft 0.1 · 30 Aug 2026 · for Lois McKellar, Mel Barlow and the Content & Clinical
Governance Group · Confidential — ACU & BoR**

*The pilot exists to produce a result a Victorian maternity service will buy on, and a
paper the discipline will cite. Those two goals constrain the design more than the research
question does. This draft is built on the international evidence review of 29 Aug — every
design choice below has a reason, and the reasons are stated.*

**Nothing in this protocol is enacted until HREC approval and legal sign-off are in place.**

---

## 1. What the pilot must produce

Three artefacts, in priority order:

1. **A publishable evaluation** with pre-declared success criteria — the asset that
   converts every subsequent sales conversation from a demonstration into an outcomes
   discussion.
2. **An equity finding that survives scrutiny** — stratified, not averaged.
3. **A TRL 5→6 evidence trail** — the Companion validated in a relevant environment.

## 2. Sites and cohort

**Two Victorian sites**, one metropolitan and one regional, named through ACU Midwifery.
The regional site is not a nice-to-have: it is the equity claim, and Innovation Victoria
will read it as such.

**Cohort:** women from **20 weeks gestation to 12 months postnatal**, consistent with the
product definition.

**Selection — the single most important design decision.** Do **not** recruit an unselected
antenatal population. The cautionary case in this literature is a postpartum chatbot RCT
that reported 91% satisfaction and 74% two-week use, and a limited effect, because the
sample did not screen positive at baseline. **High satisfaction with no effect size is
unsellable and unpublishable at once.** Recruit where there is room to move — the highest-
retention study in the perinatal engagement review was the one with the most distressed
cohort at baseline. Selection criteria to be set with the Governance Group; the principle
is need, not convenience.

**Target n:** to be set by the Group against the primary outcome's power requirement, not
by what is easy to recruit.

## 3. Enrolment — the design choice that decides retention

The evidence here is unambiguous and it should govern the protocol:

| Enrolment route | Median retention |
|---|---|
| Self-enrolment | **0 days** (IQR 0–2) |
| Clinic-enrolled, no tangible package | 0 days (IQR 0–33) |
| **Clinic-enrolled with a tangible package** | **167 days** (IQR 96–248) |

*Sander et al., npj Digital Medicine 2025 — SMART start feasibility study*

Therefore:

1. **Enrolment happens in clinic, at a scheduled appointment, by a midwife.** Never a QR
   code on a poster, never a link in a discharge pack.
2. **Enrolment is an event, not a link.** The welcome pack already exists as a design
   intention in the IP plan — box, journal, magnet. **Build it and hand it over at
   enrolment.** This is the single highest-leverage cheap intervention in the protocol.
3. **The midwife introduces Elena by name**, in the room, in under a minute. Endorsement
   from the woman's own clinician is the transfer of trust the product cannot manufacture.
4. Enrolment code issued at that moment — the platform already supports this.

## 4. Outcomes

**Primary.** Maternal self-efficacy at 6 weeks postpartum, using a validated instrument
chosen by the Governance Group. Rationale: it is the construct the product is designed to
move, it is measurable in the pilot window, and it is what a Director of Nursing recognises.

**Secondary.**
- Maternal confidence and anxiety at enrolment, 6 weeks and 12 weeks
- Self-reported help-seeking appropriateness — did she contact her care team when she
  should have, and not when she shouldn't
- Service-contact patterns where the service can supply them (avoidable presentations is
  the number that buys, but it needs the service's data and honest limitations)
- Breastfeeding continuation where relevant to the cohort

**Engagement — reported against the field's benchmark, not in isolation.** Pooled dropout
in app-based interventions is 43% (95% CI 29–57). Report: enrolment-to-first-use,
7-day, 30-day and 90-day retention, sessions per active user, and time-of-day distribution.
**Pre-declare the retention definition and the denominator before enrolment opens.**

**Enactment — the differentiator.** Only 38% of studies in this field measure whether women
used what they learned. Include it: a short structured item at 6 and 12 weeks on whether
the woman applied specific guidance. Measuring enactment puts Kindred Care above the
field's own reporting standard and aligns directly with the DEFINE reporting-guideline work.

**Warmth trap — measured, with a threshold.** The dependence risk is the founder's own
published research concern and it cannot be left unmeasured in the venture's own pilot.
Specify: an indicator of reliance versus confidence-building, a threshold that triggers a
design review, and what the product does if the threshold is crossed. The Group sets the
instrument.

## 5. Stratification — pre-specified, not post hoc

Analysis stratified by **education, parity, and digital confidence**, specified before
data collection.

The reason is uncomfortable and should be stated in the protocol rather than discovered in
the results. Adherence in this literature runs **higher** among tech-confident, higher-
educated, first-time mothers and **lower** among women who already have children. Kindred
Care is being funded partly on an equity promise. **An unstratified evaluation will produce
a regressive result and undercut the claim.** Stratified, an intervention that narrows the
adherence gap is a genuinely publishable finding and a defensible moat.

Add rural/metropolitan and CALD/First Nations strata where numbers permit; report honestly
where they do not.

## 6. Data collection and who does it

Every pilot in this literature that lost its data lost it because collection was somebody's
spare-time job. **Name the accountable data collector at each site in the pilot agreement**,
with an agreed time allocation. Where the venture can carry the burden instead of the
service, it should.

Automated: platform engagement telemetry, retrieval quality, safety-trigger events.
Human: the outcome instruments at enrolment, 6 weeks and 12 weeks.
Qualitative: exit interviews with a purposive subsample — the perinatal engagement review
found only 31% of studies conducted them, and they carry the insight the numbers cannot.

## 7. Safety during the pilot

Standing controls apply and must be evidenced before the first participant: deterministic
T1/T2/T3 triggers with T3 unsuppressible; dual-agent clinical supervision on every
response; autonomy-first escalation to the woman's nominated care team; the escalation
directory pointed at the **real services** at each site, not test data; the safety help
sheet; and an incident log with a named reviewer and a review cadence.

A **Clinical Safety Officer** is nominated for the pilot period, per the Business Plan.

**Consented outreach defaults off.** If a site wants it enabled, that is a separate
conversation with its own consent basis.

## 8. Ethics and consent — the hard prerequisite

HREC approval is coordinated by ACU and is **open**. Legal sign-off on the privacy notice
and terms is **open**. Neither is optional and neither can be worked around.

**Consent must cover research use at the point of collection.** It cannot be retrofitted.
Any transcript collected under consent that does not cover research use is unusable for
publication and unusable for PhD Paper 5 — permanently. This is the single most expensive
mistake available in the whole programme and it is entirely avoidable by sequencing.

Separate consent layers: product use, research participation, and any future secondary
analysis.

## 9. Pre-declaration

Before enrolment opens, and agreed in writing with each site:

- The primary outcome and its instrument
- The success criteria, in numbers
- The retention definition and denominator
- The stratification variables
- The analysis plan
- What will be published, by whom, and on what timeline
- What happens if the result is negative — **it is published anyway**

That last line belongs in the pilot agreement. A venture that reserves the right to
suppress its own negative findings has no evidence moat, only marketing.

## 10. From pilot to purchase

The pilot agreement names the conversion conversation and its date at the outset. Pilots
that drift become permanent free deployments, and free pilots in Australian health prove
curiosity rather than budget. Size the first pilot as a **fixed fee including the
evaluation**, beneath the service's direct-engagement procurement threshold — never as a
discount off list.
