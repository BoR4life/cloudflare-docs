## 1. Why this venture belongs to this university

Kindred Care is not a technology company that happens to have found a university partner.
It is a commercialisation of ACU's own scholarship — midwifery, nursing and psychology —
delivered into the population ACU's health faculty exists to serve.

The proposition rests on something only a university can supply. Any competent engineering
team can build a conversational product; almost none can put a faculty's clinical
authority behind every answer it gives. **The academic governance layer is not a compliance
overhead on this venture — it is the product's defensibility.** Remove ACU and Kindred Care
becomes an ordinary maternal app; keep ACU and it becomes the governed alternative a health
service can sanction.

## 2. Alignment to the strategic pillars

*Russell — the headings below are placeholders for ACU 2033's actual pillar names.
Substitute them and the mapping should hold.*

**Mission and the common good.** The venture's beneficiaries are the mothers least served
by the current model: rural and regional women, CALD and First Nations families, single
parents, young mothers, and women navigating loss. The Companion's cultural adaptation
layer and its ten-avatar multicultural library exist for that reason. Equity here is a
design commitment carried into the evaluation design, where outcomes are stratified rather
than averaged — so the venture can be held to the claim rather than merely make it.

**Research with impact.** Kindred Care converts ACU midwifery scholarship into a deployed
instrument and returns evidence to the discipline. A PhD runs in parallel under Professor
Robert Tai's supervision, with Kindred Care-aligned outcomes separated from non-aligned
ones. Pilot evaluations are designed for publication from the outset. ACU retains research
and teaching licences over the knowledge base, the evaluation frameworks and the persona
design **regardless of who ultimately owns the company**.

**Learning and teaching.** The same governed Companion, re-skinned as a standardised
client, allows midwifery students to rehearse dozens of real-world conversations on demand
— with learning analytics for faculty and without a placement bottleneck. This is a direct
teaching asset for ACU's own programs before it is a commercial product for anyone else.

**Partnerships and engagement.** The venture deepens ACU's standing with Victorian
maternity services, the Australian College of Midwives, and the state's clinical quality
infrastructure — relationships that serve the faculty whether or not the company succeeds.

**Sustainability of the institution.** A commercialisation return, and a demonstrated
pathway for translating health-faculty research into practice.

## 3. Alignment to risk appetite

*Russell — please replace the appetite characterisations with ACU's actual scale.*

| Risk domain | Exposure | Treatment | Residual |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Clinical and patient safety** | The product speaks to women in a vulnerable period | Education-only, enforced architecturally not by policy; cannot diagnose, triage or interpret symptoms; deterministic crisis triggers that bypass the model entirely; dual-agent clinical supervision of every response; autonomy-first escalation to the woman's own care team | **Low** — and evidenced by adversarial test rather than assertion |
| **Regulatory** | Software in a health context can fall within TGA device regulation | Non-clinical scope maintained deliberately; boundary tests documented; written classification position being obtained before any service pilot | **Low, pending the written position** |
| **Reputational** | ACU's name on an AI product for new mothers | ACU controls content approval; no content reaches a mother without academic sign-off; all external ACU-naming material sighted by the Enterprise Office; the venture publishes its own limitations | **Low–moderate**, actively managed |
| **Research integrity** | The founder is both CEO and a Student Investigator | Declared on the HREC application; KC-aligned and non-aligned PhD outcomes separated; publication protocol prevents suppression of negative findings, surviving change of control | **Moderate, declared** — managed rather than eliminated, which is the honest position |
| **Financial** | ACU co-investment at risk | Staged: scoping, then $150k at POC, then $500k at commercialisation, each gated on evidence. No commitment beyond the current stage at any point | **Moderate** and bounded by design |
| **Privacy and data** | Health-adjacent personal information | Australian-resident infrastructure; transcripts only with no recording, enforced in CI; consent at point of collection covering research use; tenant isolation at database level | **Low, pending legal sign-off** |
| **Delivery** | Pre-revenue venture with a small team | Instrumented TRL gates; a recruited lead at incorporation; a programme register with named owners and a critical path | **Moderate** |

**Two exposures ACU should see plainly.** The ACU-side cash match of $650,000 across both
rounds has no identified source; and the venture's founder carries concurrent
commitments, mitigated by a recruited lead at incorporation. Both are on the venture's own
risk register and neither is being presented as solved.

## 4. What ACU is asked to accept, and what it is not

**Accept:** a staged co-investment gated at each step; a licence of ACU background IP into
the venture rather than an assignment; and the reputational association of ACU's name with
a governed, education-only product.

**Not asked to accept:** operational delivery, clinical liability, long-term capital
commitment, or any obligation beyond the current stage. Progression past the pilot is
subject to a formal scale decision gate that binds nobody in advance.

## 5. Standing conditions

ACU retains content approval authority; the education-only boundary is not negotiable in
any commercial negotiation; ACU's research and teaching licences survive change of
control; the publication protocol survives change of control; and all external material
naming ACU is sighted by the Enterprise Office before release.
