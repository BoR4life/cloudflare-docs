## The three slip scenarios, and what to do

**If the cash match has no dated position by 25 Sep.** Do not let UCAC be the forum where
it surfaces. Ask Russell to raise it at the Steering Committee on 16 Sept as an explicit
question to the committee — *"where inside ACU does this realistically come from?"* A
committee asked for help gives resources; a committee shown a hole late gives a deferral.

**If the Heads of Agreement is not drafted by 25 Sep.** Offer to draft the issues list so
it becomes a review task for ACU Legal rather than a drafting task. That converts weeks
into days and it is entirely within our gift.

**If the TRL 4 dossier slips.** It does not stop UCAC. The venture is going to UCAC at
**TRL 3, deliberately and honestly**, with the POC as the funded climb. The dossier
strengthens the case; its absence does not invalidate it. Do not let a schedule slip
become a reason to overclaim the level.
