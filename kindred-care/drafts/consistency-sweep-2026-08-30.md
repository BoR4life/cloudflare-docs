# The consistency sweep — executed

**30 Aug 2026.** Held since 29 Aug pending decisions that are now made: the **Pricing Model's
schedule is canonical**, ownership is a **holding line pending ACU's draft**. Both unblock
the sweep.

**This is not another review. It is the change list**, document by document, ready to work
through. Every row cites `canonical-numbers.md` as the target.

**Why it matters more than it sounds.** On 30 Aug I costed the Business Plan's prices against
the Pricing Model's cohorts and produced a conclusion that was wrong in both magnitude and
direction. That is precisely what an inconsistent pack does to a careful reader — and a
diligence analyst has less context than I did, not more.

## Sequence

1. **Pricing Model first.** It is the cost engine everything else derives from, and its own
   internal inconsistencies (uptake cell, enrolled period, the backwards flag) must be fixed
   before other documents are aligned to it.
2. **Then canonical-numbers**, if the Pricing Model fixes move any figure.
3. **Then the Business Plan and Forecast** — the two most-read documents.
4. **Then deck, GTM, Product Overview, Unit Economics.**
5. **Then the greps.** Fast, and they catch what a structured pass misses.

**Standing rule going forward:** a change to any canonical row propagates the same week, and
the source column is updated. The sweep is not a one-off — it is the discipline that stops
this recurring.
