at once, undisclosed.

## The three things to learn, conversationally

1. **What does she want?** Ask it straight: "If this worked perfectly for you, what would

   assessment.
2. **The COVID app.** Warm and early: *"Lois mentioned you built your own education app

   who funded it, who owns it, whether it still exists, whether anyone else has rights.
   This is background IP and it is far cheaper asked now than found at diligence.
3. **Where does she work now?** She writes from a personal address. If she is currently
   employed by a Victorian health service, Russell's category-C confidentiality process
