# Conflict of interest and related-party register

**Stood up 31 Aug 2026.** The agent has been saying "declare it on the COI register" since 30
August and no register existed. This is it.

**Why it matters more than it sounds.** Nothing on this page is misconduct. Founders use
their networks, advisors make introductions, and technical co-founders bring their own
companies. **The damage is never the interest — it is the interest an investor discovers
that the pack did not disclose.** A declared interest is governance; an undeclared one found
at diligence is a credibility event.

**Rule:** every prospect is recorded here at rung 0 — who introduced, what their interest is,
which pathway it counts as — **before the approach**, not at diligence.
